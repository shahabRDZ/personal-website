A few things about me and what I do.

I'm a Computer Engineering graduate from Sharif University of Technology — one of the most competitive engineering programs in the region. Over the past several years, I've worked across the full spectrum of software development: from building backend APIs at scale, to designing cloud-native architectures on AWS, to shipping cross-platform mobile apps with Flutter.

I believe great software starts with understanding the problem deeply before writing a single line of code. Whether I'm setting up CI/CD pipelines, optimizing database queries, or prototyping a new mobile experience, I care about building things that are reliable, maintainable, and genuinely useful.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me exploring new AI tools, reading about distributed systems, or mentoring junior developers — something I've done since my time as a TA at Sharif.
